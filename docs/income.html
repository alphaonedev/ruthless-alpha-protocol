Reproducible mapping

Cash yield is the sum of regular cash distributions with ex-dates in the trailing 12 months divided by the latest price; special distributions are excluded. Upside is the larger of one-year adjusted return and three-year CAGR; funds younger than three years use the history available. The kill state is −40%, with the displayed probability as an explicit assumption rather than a measurement. Kelly is capped at 100%; half Kelly is the decision cap, and standalone fractions ignore cross-position correlation. MPW (no Alpaca prices after 30 January 2026) and NUSI (no trades in the past year) were excluded.
